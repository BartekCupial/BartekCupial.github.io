Bartłomiej Cupiał

Hello there! I'm Bartek, a PhD student at the University of Warsaw, supervised by Piotr Miłoś. My research sits at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and multi-agent coordination.

I work on efficient and adaptive AI agents that learn when to plan, reason, and collaborate. During my PhD, I've had the pleasure of working with Tim Rocktäschel and the UCL DARK Lab on long-horizon LLM agents. I've also interned at Sakana AI with Jeffrey Seely, working on decentralized, collaborative multi-agent systems, and at Google under Jarek Mickiewicz, working on improving instruction following in LLMs.

When I'm not buried in research papers, you might find me battling it out in NetHack (though I'll admit, my AI is catching up to my skills). I'm always up for a good chat, so feel free to reach out!
